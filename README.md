## 🚀 Things I've been building

### 🩸 [RaktJaal](https://github.com/samsara0902/RaktJaal)
A connected network for blood donors and people who need blood. The idea is simple: when someone urgently needs blood, they shouldn't have to scroll through endless messages to find a donor. RaktJaal links the two sides in one place so help can reach people faster.

### ⚖️ [Casebox](https://github.com/samsara0902/Casebox)
A full-stack legal case management platform built with Django and React. It gives admins, advocates, judges and clients their own role-based access, keeps track of hearings automatically, and has internal collaboration tools so legal teams can work together without things slipping through the cracks.
